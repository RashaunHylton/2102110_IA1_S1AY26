Favourite AI Business Idea — AI Education Assistant

The AI Education Assistant concept uses artificial intelligence to help students learn faster by providing personalized feedback, adaptive study plans, and 24/7 support. In my world, this could be applied to help computing students better understand web development concepts and coding practices.

Such tools can identify weak areas and suggest custom exercises, improving self-paced learning. However, ethical issues such as data privacy, over-reliance on technology, and biased algorithms must be addressed. These can be mitigated through transparent AI systems, privacy policies, and human oversight.

An example of an existing app in this area is Khan Academy, which uses AI to tailor lessons to each student’s needs.
